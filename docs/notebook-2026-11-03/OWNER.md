# Using PLUB tomorrow

Open https://co.nonarkara.org on your phone.

1. Search a place, open it and tap **I've been here**. Date the visit, add a note/photo and save.
2. Open **Notes**. Edit or search your visits. They work as a device notebook without an account.
3. Open **Cloud notebook** and create an account. Download the private notebook key and keep it somewhere safe. It is your sign-in on another device; anyone holding it can open that cloud notebook.
4. Tap **Back up** beside a visit to save a private cloud copy. On another device, sign in with the key and choose **Restore / manage**. Cloud and device copies change only when you explicitly save/restore them.
5. Use **Share…** only for a recommendation you want public. Review the note; photos are off until selected. The original stays in your private notebook.
6. Research is an optional checkbox in **Research & account**. Turning it off excludes saved visits from future research results. No background travel tracking is implemented.

For owner moderation, open macOS **Keychain Access**, search `co.nonarkara.org · PLUB moderation`, and reveal/copy the password. In PLUB, open **Notes → Moderation · owner**, paste the key and open the queue. You can inspect reported public photos, keep a post visible or remove it. The key is separate from notebook sign-in and is never embedded in the site.

Use your browser's **Install app / Add to Home Screen** command to put PLUB on your phone's home screen. Maps/cloud still need internet. Google sign-in and a Google Play listing are later steps.

Export before clearing a browser. Sign-out does not erase device copies; **Clear this device** is separate. Private cloud data, public posts and account deletion have their own controls. A lost notebook key cannot be recovered; keep its file private.
