# Choosing a base, an outing, and a place to work

These six scenarios are design hypotheses, grounded in the owner's Ramkhamhaeng, BTS and Sam Yan examples and official venue evidence. Language is a preference. Transport, budget, identity requirements, trip length and work needs are choices in both languages. Thailand's 2024 Digital Outlook survey documents several frequencies of online work from home. Its published sample is unweighted and cannot establish a Bangkok growth rate. [NSO ICT indicators](https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Indicators-ICT/The-2024-ICT-Indicators/58/) · [Thammasat Bangkok WFH study](https://ethesisarchive.library.tu.ac.th/thesis/2024/TU_2024_6316120168_15311_30473.pdf)

| Decision | Thai scenario | Non-Thai scenario | What PLUB helps decide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where to live | A Thai freelancer moves out of a family home, still drives to help parents, and wants a workable Tuesday near Bang Kapi or Ramkhamhaeng. | A newcomer compares two temporary bases before signing a lease and wants backup work options reachable from rail. | Compare identical 800 m circles, rail proximity, mapped cafés, co-working and libraries. Open each anchor and try a real work session before committing. Rent, noise, street access, flood exposure and routine trip times need separate checks. |
| Where to go today | After a barber appointment, a driver wants somewhere useful to wait out traffic. Another day they might want a coffee stroll with friends. | A visitor with an open afternoon wants to explore a district without needing to plan every stop. | Pick an area, launch the drive-and-parking search, open the map or start a café hop. Straight lines and mapped parking are qualified. The directions provider handles live routing and traffic. |
| Where to work | A hybrid worker has two hours in Sam Yan and needs a socket for the laptop. A quiet reading session has different needs. | A remote worker has an online meeting and wants published hours, Wi-Fi terms and a backup near rail. They may need to ask whether an ID rule applies to them. | Choose work type, travel mode and time. Use published admission evidence, and check missing facilities at the venue. Keep the fallback action beside the shortlisted options. |

The next loop starts after a useful visit. A private note helps the visitor decide whether to return. A neighbourhood stamp records a place explored. A voluntary review can answer a concrete question for the next person, such as whether guests can find a socket or need an admission pass. Sharing stays in the existing explicit preview-and-publish flow. No points depend on posting, and no public leaderboard or passive location tracking is involved.

## Source plan

| Evidence | Use | Collection and limits |
|---|---|---|
| OSM and Overture snapshots | Catalogue identities, venue counts, eligible rail points | Reuse pinned inputs, retain attribution, and link to exact station records. Counts reflect coverage in the fixed circles. Distances are anchor to station, straight line. [OSM licensing](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [Overture Places](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/) |
| Operator pages | Opening hours, pass cost and admission terms | Fetch branch-specific pages and store reviewed facts, check date and source. Unknown amenities remain unknown. New intake starts with TCDC Bangkok's hours and day pass, and BACC Art Library's Wi-Fi admission condition. [TCDC hours](https://library.tcdc.or.th/page/visit/bkk_contact) · [TCDC day pass](https://www.tcdc.or.th/en/tcdc-bangkok/service/membership) · [BACC Art Library](https://www.bacc.or.th/en/plan-your-visit/art-library/) |
| Bangkok Open Data and operator feeds | Future access context, parks and service status | Check the licence, actual data age and geographic coverage per dataset before ingestion. [BKK Data Platform](https://opendata.bangkok.go.th/) |
| Visitor notes | Seat-specific Wi-Fi, outlets, noise and practical access | Retain dates and visitor attribution, with optional publication. These are individual observations. |

## Pilot test

Ask Thai- and English-preferring participants with mixed driving and rail habits to choose between two bases, find something to do this afternoon, and choose a place for a two-hour laptop session. Measure time to a defensible choice, whether they correctly identify an unknown facility, and whether the saved note helps them on a later visit. Ask what they would publish after the visit. These are proposed field tests, separate from browser verification.
